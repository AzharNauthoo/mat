FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Transfers are rides that you and your crew take from the airport to your accommodation. Normally, you meet the driver outside of the arrival hall of the airport. He will pick-up and load your luggage in the vehicle. Then, he will take you to your hotel/ destination.

Our airport transportation services are exclusively private for your family or crew. Your vehicle is never shared with other passengers.

A transfer for 2 people in Mauritius normally costs around €30 to €50. These rates also depend on the destination and number of people to carry.

The quantity of luggage you can bring is determined by the number of people traveling and the vehicle you are in. By default, each passenger is allowed one suitcase and one bag. We recognize that you may have extra luggage so in that situation, please clarify your needs when making your reservation, and an appropriate ride dedicated to your needs will be assigned to you. If you need baby seats, please let us know when you book. Your first baby seat is free, and each extra seat costs €10 per transfer.

There is no payment in advance for any of our transfers. Cancellations and changes can be made by email at alan@mauritiusairport-transfers.com or contacting us at +230 5826 4642. For more information, contact us directly.!

Proceed to the exit (of the arrival lounge) after completing customs. Your driver will be waiting for you here for the pick-up. He'll be carrying a board or paper with your name printed on it. Simply provide him with your electronic receipt or booking reference, and he will direct you to your vehicle.

There is no deposit and cancellation is always free. We do request a confirmation 48 hours before your arrival, just notify us then.

We track airplane arrival times on a daily basis and arrange accodingly. Please provide us with your flight details for us to track your arrival schedule after requesting a quote.

Depending on the destination and number of people to carry, taxi prices from the airport is €30 for regions around Mahebourg. For other regions in Mauritius, the price is normally higher especially for hotels in the North of the island.

After you get to your location, you will pay your driver in cash. You can pay in dollars, euros, pounds and mauritian rupees. If you schedule numerous excursions with us, we advise you to just pay the percentage of the price that applies to the tour you take.

We offer multiple sightseeing tours and activities around the island. The sightseeing tours include North Tour, South West Tour, South East Tour and Port Louis Tour. The activities include Catamaran And Swim With Dolphins. Our chauffeurs will show you a booklet of the best spots during your airport transfer.
