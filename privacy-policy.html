2. The personal data we collect

When you ask for a quote or book a transfer

Our quote and booking forms can ask for:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • your phone number
  • your flight number
  • the pickup place and the destination, for example your hotel or address
  • the date and time of your transfer or arrival
  • the number of adults and children in your group
  • the number of baby seats you need
  • the number of suitcases and bags
  • the vehicle you choose and the price we quote
  • whether we can contact you on WhatsApp
  • any special request, for example extra luggage, golf bags or surfboards

Some forms ask for less. For example, our short quote forms ask only for your trip details and your email address.

We need most of this information to arrange your transfer. Without the required details, we cannot give you a quote or book your transfer. Special requests are optional.

Information about health

A special request can include information about health, for example that someone in your group uses a wheelchair. Please tell us only what we need to plan your transfer. We use this information only to choose the right vehicle and to help you on the day. We share it only with your driver.

If you give us this information, you agree that we use it for this purpose. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

When you contact us

If you email us, call us or message us on WhatsApp, we receive your name, your phone number or email address, and your messages. On WhatsApp, we can also see your profile name and photo if you share them.

When you visit our website

  • Our web hosting provider records technical data when your browser loads our pages: your IP address, your browser type, the pages you visit, and the date and time. [CONFIRM: hosting provider name]
  • Google collects data through cookies and similar technology. See section 8, Cookies.
  • Our quote form puts your trip details (transfer type, destination and number of passengers) in the address of the results page. Google can see this page address when it measures visits.

On the day of your transfer

Your driver waits for you at the airport with a sign showing your name. The driver may call or message you if they cannot find you.

You pay your driver in cash after the ride. We do not collect card or bank details.

Information about other people

If you book for other people, please tell them about this policy.

3. Why we use your data

We use your personal data only for the reasons below. The law says we need a legal reason (a "legal basis") for each use. We show it in brackets.

  • To answer your questions and give you a quote (because you asked us to, before you book).
  • To book and carry out your transfer: to confirm your booking, send your receipt, send you a reminder about 48 hours before you arrive, check your flight time, and give your details to your driver (to carry out our contract with you).
  • To contact you about changes, delays or problems with your transfer (to carry out our contract with you).
  • To use health information that you give us in a special request (your explicit consent).
  • To keep records for accounting and tax (to meet our legal obligations).
  • To deal with complaints and legal claims (our legitimate interest in protecting our business).
  • To keep our website working and safe (our legitimate interest in running a secure website).
  • To measure our Google ads and understand how visitors use our website (your consent to cookies).

We do not send newsletters or advertising emails. [CONFIRM: no marketing emails or messages are sent]

We do not make decisions about you by automated means only, and we do not use profiling that affects you in a significant way.

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Our website uses cookies from Google:

  • Google Ads (on most pages). These cookies tell us if a visit came from one of our Google ads and if it led to a booking request. Google may also use them for advertising. Example: _gcl_au. They last up to 90 days.
  • Google Analytics (on our home page). These cookies count visits and show us how visitors use our website. Example: _ga. They last up to 2 years.

We use these cookies only if you accept them. When you first visit our website, we ask for your choice. You can change your choice at any time. [CONFIRM: a cookie consent banner is live on the website. Do not publish this paragraph until it is.]

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: ask if we hold data about you, and get a free copy.
  • Correction: ask us to correct data that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Deletion: ask us to delete your data.
  • Restriction: ask us to limit how we use your data, for example while we check if it is correct.
  • Objection: object to how we use your data. You can always object to direct marketing.
  • Withdraw consent: where we use your data because you agreed, for example for cookies or health information, you can change your mind at any time. This does not affect what we did before.
  • A human decision: not be subject to a decision made only by automated means that has legal or similar serious effects on you.
  • Data portability (customers in the EU and the UK): get the data you gave us in a common electronic format, or ask us to send it to another company.

To use your rights, send us a request in writing by email or WhatsApp. We may ask you to prove your identity. We will reply within one month. If your request is complex, we may need one more month, and we will tell you. This is free, unless a request is clearly excessive.

Another person can make a request for you if you give them written permission. A parent or guardian can make a request for a child.

Sometimes we cannot delete data, for example when the law says we must keep it. If so, we will tell you why.
