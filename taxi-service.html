FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How does the booking system work ?

1. Request a quote
2. Accept quote and fill additional info
3. You are booked !
4. A gentle reminder will be sent to you 48 hours for the booking.

Are the fares fully inclusive or will there be additional fees ?

No hidden fees to hassle you and no unfriendly surprise. We take a lot of care to provide exceptional service. The quoted price is the final price and everything in the service is included in the final quoted price.

How to change the time and date for a taxi transfer ?

Contact the office via WhatsApp or phone on +230 5826 4642. We will gladly take care of your request in seconds.

Are baby seats included ?

Your first baby seat is free on every transfer. Each extra baby seat costs €10 per transfer. Just tell us how many you need when you book.

What happens if I do not receive a receipt by mail ?

These receipts are electronically generated and sent.
First thing, check your spam folder.
If you cannot find it, try entering your details again in the quote form.
You can also contact us directly on whatsapp, e-mail or phone. We will sort out your issue immediately

Can I pay with my credit card ?

Unfortunately, our services are fully paid in cash but if you need to find an ATM, our drivers will gladly show you the closest ones near you at any location.

How to pay for the transfer?

After you get at your location, you will pay your driver in cash. You can pay in dollars/ euros/ pounds/ mur. If you schedule numerous excursions with us, we advise you just pay the percentage of the price that applies to the tour you take. 

What additional services do you offer?

We offer multiple tours around the island. Our chauffeurs will show you a booklet of our best hand-picked spots during your airport transfer
