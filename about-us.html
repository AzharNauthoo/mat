About us Mauritius Airport Transfers Corp.

Mauritius Airport Transfers was founded in 2010 to fulfill our desire for assisting our customers in finding a quick, convenient, and dependable transportation to their hotel. We couldn't have guessed how far we'd come when we first began out, originally only specializing in Taxi cabs around the island. We are a group of taxi drivers that have been working in the tourism sector for a long period, providing taxis, excursions, and transfers to customers from all over the world.

Our duty is to employ our cab and transfer services at the SSR International Airport and our primary goal as a taxi and transfer service provider from the SSR International Airport in Plaisance, Mauritius, is to complement your vacation or business travels with providing ease and comfort of travel

Why Choose Mauritius Airport Transfers ?

We are a committed organization with the goal of providing the greatest quality service to our esteemed clients. For the standard of service we provide, our value is exceptional. Our range of vehicles covers for all our clients needs from 1 person to 25 people.

We provide emergency coverage across Mauritius 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Our vehicles are completely insured and licensed and our drivers are well trained. We do not accept prior payment for transfers, pay after your ride We provide the most adaptable service possible; you may quickly amend or cancel your transfers, and we don't ask why you're doing it.
