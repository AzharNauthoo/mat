Useful Information

Where To Find Your Driver At The Airport?

After Clearing Customs, Proceed Out Of The Arrival Lounge And Go Through EXIT GATE 7. Your Driver will Be Waiting Holding A Sign With Your Name On It.

Payment For Transfer

Payment Is Made After You Have Reached Your Hotel Or Destination. You can Pay In Euro, Dollar and Mauritian Rupee. If You Need An ATM, Your Driver Will Show You At The Airport.

Bookings On Behalf Of Other People

Please Make Sure To Write The NAME OF THE LEAD TRAVELLER In The Booking Form So That We Can Print A Sign With His Name On It And He Can Easily Find Us At The Airport.
